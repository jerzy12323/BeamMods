Legal information

DMCA notice.

BeamMods respects copyright and intellectual property rights. If you believe a mod, image, description or other material on the platform infringes your copyright, you can send a formal DMCA notice for review.

What to include

Provide your full name, contact details, identification of the copyrighted work, the direct BeamMods URL of the reported material, a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized, and a statement that the information is accurate under penalty of perjury.

Send notices to beammodshub@gmail.com with the subject line DMCA takedown request. We may contact the uploader for clarification and will review complete notices as promptly as possible.

Please do not submit false or incomplete claims. If you are the uploader and believe content was removed in error, include the original mod URL and an explanation when contacting us about a counter-notice.

The BeamMods process

How it works.

BeamMods keeps publishing simple for creators and trustworthy for players. Follow four clear steps from your first sign-in to a live community release.

01

Create your account

Sign in with a local BeamMods account or use Google. Your profile and published mods stay connected to your account.

02

Prepare your upload

Add the mod name, category, creator name, versions, configs, description, ZIP file and up to five preview images. The file size is read automatically.

03

Owner review

New submissions remain private until the owner checks the preview, description, download source and technical details. You can see the pending status in your dashboard.

04

Publish and share

After approval, the mod becomes visible in the public library. Players can open its details, read your description and follow the download source.
