The BeamMods process

How it works.

BeamMods keeps publishing simple for creators and trustworthy for players. Follow four clear steps from your first sign-in to a live community release.

01

Create your account

Sign in with a local BeamMods account or use Google. Your profile and published mods stay connected to your account.

02

Prepare your upload

Add the mod name, category, creator name, versions, configs, description, ZIP file and up to five preview images. The file size is read automatically.

03

Owner review

New submissions remain private until the owner checks the preview, description, download source and technical details. You can see the pending status in your dashboard.

04

Publish and share

After approval, the mod becomes visible in the public library. Players can open its details, read your description and follow the download source.
